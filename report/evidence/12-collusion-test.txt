Documented limitations:
  - Resistance is probabilistic and parameterized for small coalitions
    (Tardos-inspired toy, 512-bit codewords, coalition size 2-3)
  - Large coalitions, JPEG re-encoding, resize, and crop destroy the mark
    (evidence 13 section 4: 0/30 survival for each of those transforms)

    client code path; it is not server attestation and a patched client can
    suppress or forge marks
