Method:
  The harness generated a fresh AES-256-GCM key and 96-bit nonce, encrypted the
  file locally, uploaded it with a password split (salt + KDF iterations) and two
  recipients, downloaded by share id, reconstructed K = R XOR W, decrypted with
  the returned nonce, and compared SHA-256 digests and exact bytes.
